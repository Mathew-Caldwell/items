# Enchantments
**Scythe** (Hoe Levels I-V)  
Reforges the hoe into a real weapon. Its damage and attack speed are raised to match the sword of the chosen tier: I is wood or gold, II stone, III iron, IV diamond, V netherite.  
<img width="300" height="68" alt="2026-10-07_20 01 09_edited" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c2047c4d-d4d7-4aec-9db0-06bcdd09783c" />  

**Bleed** (Hoe Levels I-V)  
Hits open a wound that deals damage once a second, ignoring armor. It lasts 3 to 7 seconds depending on level. Levels I-III deal ½ a heart per second and IV-V deal a full heart. Hitting again refreshes the bleed without stacking it.  
<img width="300" height="68" alt="2026-10-07_19 45 10_edited" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0d08ba68-b290-4dd6-bd8f-deb2e7b58686" />  

**Pierce** (Hoe Levels I-V)  
Hits ignore a portion of the target's armor. Each level ignores another 15%, up to 75% at level V.  
<img width="300" height="68" alt="2026-10-07_19 57 59_edited" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4ed8888d-671d-4512-aea1-553387096282" />  

**Zeus Wrath** (Hoe Levels I-III)
