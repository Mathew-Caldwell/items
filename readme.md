# Enchantments
**Scythe** (Hoe Levels I-V)  
Reforges the hoe into a real weapon. Its damage and attack speed are raised to match the sword of the chosen tier: I is wood or gold, II stone, III iron, IV diamond, V netherite.

**Bleed** (Hoe Levels I-V)  
Hits open a wound that deals damage once a second, ignoring armor. It lasts 3 to 7 seconds depending on level. Levels I-III deal ½ a heart per second and IV-V deal a full heart. Hitting again refreshes the bleed without stacking it.

**Pierce** (Hoe Levels I-V)  
Hits ignore a portion of the target's armor. Each level ignores another 15%, up to 75% at level V.

**Zeus Wrath** (Hoe Levels I-III)  
Calls lightning down on whatever you hit. Each level adds one more bolt. Incompatible with Disarm

**Steal** (Hoe Levels I-III)  
Hits have a 33%, 50% or 66% chance to strip every status effect from the target and give them to you, keeping their strength and remaining duration.

**Executioner** (Hoe Levels I-V)  
Finishes off weakened targets. When your hit leaves the victim below 20% to 40% health (rising with level), you deal bonus damage of 2 to 6 hearts.

**Totem Breaker** (Hoe Level I)  
Totems of Undying can't save a target from your hits. If a hit pops their totem, they die anyway.

**Hex** (Hoe Levels I-III)  
Curses the target for 5, 7 or 9 seconds. While hexed, they take 20%, 35% or 50% extra damage from every source, not just yours.

**Backstab** (Hoe Levels I-III)  
Hitting a target from behind deals bonus damage of 1.5, 2.5 or 3.5 hearts.

**Disarm** (Hoe Level I)  
Hits have a 30% chance to knock the item out of the target's main hand and onto the ground. Incompatible with Zeus Wrath

**Mirror Image** (Chestplate Levels I-IV)  
When hit spawns decoy copy of player. +1 decory per level

**Reflect** (Chestplatae Levels I-III)  
When hit has 5% chance to take no damage and the attacker receives all damage that would have been done. +5% chance per level

**Rigid Shock** (Chestplate Levels I-III)  
When hit has 5% chance of the attacker dropping the item in their mainhand. +5% chance per level

**Misty Step** (Leggings Level I-V)  
Double crouch to teleport up to 10 blocks. +10 blocks per level after level 1

**Overcharge** (Chestplate Level I-V)  
Builds charge as you take hits, once full your next hit releases a lightning arc which chains to nearby enimies.
|Level|Hits taken to charge|Arc damage|Chain jumps|
|-----|--------------------|----------|-----------|
|I|6|4 (2 hearts)| 1|
|II|5|6 (3 hearts)|2|
|III|4|8 (4 hearts)|3|
|IV|3|10 (5 hearts)|4|
|V|2|12 (6 hearts)|5|

**Ragnarok** (Hoe Level I-V)  
Summons large storm clouds over victim where meteors rain down. Lasts for 18s and +3s per level after level 1. Incompatible with Breath of the Frost Giant

**Breath of the Frosts Griant** (Hoe Level I-V)  
Summons ice tornado around point of contact. Lasts for 18s and +3s per level after level 1. Incompatible with Ragnarok

**Jump Booster** (Boots Level I)  
Allows the wearer to jump 3 blocks high
